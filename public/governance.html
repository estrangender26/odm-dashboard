Acceptance Stages

P1 — Provisional Acceptance
Initial acceptance when core commissioning/testing documentation is complete. Facility may begin limited operations under supervision. Not final.
P2 — Final Acceptance
Granted only when ALL verified: documentation complete, SOPs/SMPs approved, training fulfilled, digital onboarding verified, critical spares handover completed.
Critical Rule
Facilities shall not be endorsed for Final Acceptance without a fully compliant O&M Manual. All 14 TOC items and 9 annexes must be complete. Ownership transfers to Operations only after formal Final Acceptance approval. No exceptions.
