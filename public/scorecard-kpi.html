KPI Definitions & FAQ
Monthly KPI Scorecard Scope and GL Account Coverage
1. Primary Scope

The Monthly KPI Scorecard monitors maintenance performance using the approved Repair and Maintenance scope. For the current scorecard, KPI values are based only on Repair and Maintenance – Technical Equipment costs and activities.

2. Included
  • Repair and Maintenance – Technical Equipment GL
  • Maintenance activities directly related to technical equipment reliability, availability, corrective maintenance, preventive maintenance, and equipment uptime
  • Equipment-related work orders and cost entries that fall under the approved Technical Equipment R&M GL scope
  • PM and CM work orders/costs where the asset or activity belongs to the Technical Equipment maintenance scope
3. Excluded
  • Building / civil / structural repairs
  • Office facilities and general admin repairs
  • Fleet / vehicle repair and maintenance
  • IT equipment and office equipment repairs
  • Property management costs
  • Janitorial, housekeeping, security, landscaping, and non-technical facility services
  • CAPEX, project works, asset replacement projects, and construction-related costs
  • Warranty works performed by contractors where no R&M cost is booked to the Technical Equipment GL
  • Non-maintenance operating expenses
  • Any R&M GL not classified as Repair and Maintenance – Technical Equipment
4. SAP R&M GL Account Reference
SAP R&M GL Category Scorecard Treatment Notes
Repair and Maintenance – Technical Equipment Included Primary GL scope for this scorecard
Repair and Maintenance – Building / Civil / Structures Excluded Outside technical equipment scope
Repair and Maintenance – Fleet / Vehicles Excluded To be monitored separately if required
Repair and Maintenance – IT / Office Equipment Excluded Outside technical equipment maintenance scorecard
Repair and Maintenance – Property / Facilities / General Services Excluded Not part of this scorecard unless separately approved
Other R&M GL Accounts Excluded by default Include only if formally mapped to Technical Equipment scope

Exact SAP GL account codes should be confirmed against the active SAP chart of accounts. This scorecard uses the GL category/scope definition and should not assume account codes unless validated from SAP.

5. Governance Note

For monthly reporting, each business unit must ensure that imported KPI values are aligned with this GL scope. If costs or work orders include non-technical-equipment items, they should be excluded or separately identified before upload.

6. Future Expansion

Additional scorecards may be created later for Fleet, Building/Facilities, Property, IT/Office Equipment, or other R&M GL categories, but these are outside the current Technical Equipment scorecard.
